01 · What happened

The traces remain. The explanations do not.

In August 1966 the electricians Manoel Pereira da Cruz and Miguel José Viana set out from Campos dos Goytacazes for Niterói. On the hillside they were found side by side: in coats, with lead masks covering their faces, and with no visible signs of struggle.

Nearby lay a bottle of water, a notebook and several objects that looked like part of an arranged ritual. The key piece of evidence — a short note with a sequence of times and a phrase about a signal — did not explain who the signal was to come from.

“16:30 — be in place. 18:30 — take the capsule… wait for the signal”.
