Theories
are not the facts.

Choose a lens. A strong hypothesis must explain not only the masks but also the note, the water, the absence of struggle and the empty slot for a signal.

Hypothesis matrix / 04

What each theory
sees in the dark.

A score is not a verdictThe bars show not probability but the amount of details explained.
01

A spiritist experiment

The men may have taken part in a ritual connected with awaiting contact or a supernatural signal. In this theory the lead is a protective material, and the times in the note are a protocol of action.

Explainsthe masks · the schedule
What supports it

The vocabulary of a “signal”, the unusual gear and the waiting time fixed in advance.

Where it breaks

There is no confirmed ritual, no witnesses or objects that point directly at spiritism.

02

A technical test

As electricians, they could have been testing an unknown device, radiation or a light effect. The masks then become an attempt to protect the eyes, and the “signal” part of a technical protocol.

Explainsprofession · protection
What supports it

A professional interest in electronics and the shape of the masks, which could have restricted light.

Where it breaks

There is no device, wiring diagram or signal source at the scene. The mask material does not prove radiation protection.

03

Poisoning or an accident

The capsule could have been a medicine or a substance that led to death. If the cause was medical, the masks and the schedule are part of a dangerous experiment, not a trace of contact.

Explainsthe “capsule” · the silence
What supports it

The mention of a capsule and the absence of visible violence agree with the version of a sudden collapse.

Where it breaks

Without proper toxicology it is impossible to identify the substance or even confirm that it was taken at all.

04

Third parties involved

Someone could have used the men’s interest in the signal to lead them up the hill. This theory treats the note as bait or an incomplete piece of someone else’s plan.

Explainsinconsistencies
What supports it

The obscure origin of the signal and the absence of an observable chain between arrival and death.

Where it breaks

There is no confirmed motive, no traces of struggle and no witness linking the place to another person.

A rule of the archive

A theory is useful
while it argues with the evidence.

The mystery does not grow weaker from the word “unknown”. On the contrary: it is the only honest signature beneath the place where the source ends.
