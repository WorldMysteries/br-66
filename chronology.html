A chronology
of waiting.

The story grows stranger when it is laid out not by theories but by the clock. Here is the route of two men, from departure to the last recorded detail.

BR—66 / TIMELOG

All marks are approximate. The sources disagree on the times.

15–16
August

Preparation

Manoel Pereira da Cruz and Miguel José Viana, who worked with electronics, get ready for a trip from Campos dos Goytacazes. They take coats, water and money — ordinary things that later begin to look like props.

testimonies about the purchases
16.08
daytime

The road to Niterói

The men reach Niterói, the city facing Rio de Janeiro across Guanabara Bay. What exactly they planned to do on the hill, none of their close ones knew for sure.

destination confirmed

“They did not look as if they had come to hide. Rather, as if they had come to wait for an agreed moment”.

16:30

Time on paper

In the notebook a sequence of time marks is found. The best-known reconstruction reads like a schedule: arrive, take the capsule, put on protection and wait for the signal. The original wording and context of the note remain disputed.

note / manuscript

A pause between actions

In the supposed schedule a “capsule” appears. No packaging or substance is found nearby. The medical answer never becomes unequivocal: a full toxicological examination could not be carried out.

a gap in the expertise
17.08
morning

Morro do Vintém

On the hillside the bodies of two men are found. Both wear coats and homemade lead masks covering the eye area. No signs of external violence are noted.

discovery site
17.08
after

The objects become evidence

The investigation logs a bottle of water, a notebook, the masks, watches and other personal items. Every object speaks of preparation — but none says who was supposed to send the signal.

scene examination
1966—
today

The case does not close the question

Without a precise cause of death and without a confident reading of the note, the story grows theories: from a spiritist experience and UFO contact to an accident. The facts stay short. The legend — long.

status: unsolved
A slice of time

The moment that cannot be verified.

Between the last mark in the note and the discovery of the bodies there are no witnesses to ask one simple question: “What exactly were they waiting for?” The whole mystery formed in this interval.
