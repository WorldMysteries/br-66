Two homemade coverings over the eye area. The strongest image of the case — and its most silent piece of evidence.

02 / OBJECT

A bottle of water

An ordinary object that makes the route concrete: people planned to spend time on the hill.

03 / OBJECT

The note

A list of times, a capsule, protection and a signal. A few lines that bred decades of guessing.

04 / OBJECT

The coats

An equally practical and sinister detail: rain gear on a day with no known bad weather.

Inventory note

Objects do not have to carry one meaning.

Water can be water. A coat — a coat. A lead mask is not necessarily a key to another civilisation. Evidence becomes a theory only after interpretation.
